# NGO Research & Data Collection in India

---

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on researching and collecting information about **10 Non-Governmental Organizations (NGOs)** operating across different social sectors in India.

The project was completed as **Task 1 of my Data Analytics Internship at InAmigos Foundation**.

The research provides an overview of different NGOs, their areas of work, headquarters, official websites, and key initiatives.

## 🎯 Objective

To understand how NGOs operate across different social sectors and develop practical skills in:

- Data Collection
- Online Research
- Data Organization
- Information Gathering
- Documentation

## 📊 Dataset Information

## 🌍 Social Sectors Covered

The research covers NGOs working in the following social sectors:

- Education
- Healthcare
- Child Welfare
- Women Empowerment
- Animal Welfare
- Environmental Conservation
- Rural Development

## 🛠️ Tools Used

- Microsoft Excel – Data collection and organization.
- Google Search – Online research and information gathering.
- Official NGO Websites – Collecting organizational information and verifying details.

## 🔍 Research Methodology

1. Identified 10 NGOs operating in different social sectors across India.
2. Visited their official websites.
3. Collected organizational information, including headquarters and areas of work.
4. Researched their key initiatives and activities.
5. Organized the collected information in Microsoft Excel.
6. Reviewed and structured the dataset for easy understanding.

## 📚 Key Learnings

Through this task, I gained practical experience in:

- Conducting online research using reliable sources.
- Collecting and organizing real-world information.
- Working with Microsoft Excel.
- Understanding the role of NGOs in social development.
- Presenting research findings in a structured format.
